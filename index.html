The pairing part

Start with dinner, not the bottle.

Most sake apps assume you already know what you want. We do it the other way around, which is how people actually eat. Tell SakeYō you're having oysters — or yakitori, or a bowl of curry — and it tells you which kind of sake goes with it, why it works, and what to say so the person behind the counter knows you mean it. This is the part people keep telling us they can't get anywhere else.

Beyond the bottle

Every good bottle comes with a story — the brewery, the family, the town it's from. We go find them, and write them down.

What SakeYō does

iKnow if you’ll like it before you buy it

Point your camera at a label, including one written entirely in Japanese, and SakeYō tells you what the bottle is and gives it a score for how well it matches your taste.

iiYour palate, mapped

SakeYō works out which styles you lean towards, such as rich, aged, dry or light, from a short quiz and the bottles you rate. You can adjust the scores yourself, and tap any style to see tasting notes and how to order it.

iiiNever forget a bottle again

The diary keeps a record of every sake you drink, with your rating and notes, the temperature, the cup, and who you were with. It also counts how many sake and breweries you’ve tried.

ivFind the good stuff nearby

The Local section lists the bars, restaurants and shops in Singapore that we go back to for good sake.

vDrink your way through Japan

The Japan section covers the sake bars, breweries, shops and restaurants we recommend across the country, with articles on places such as Sake Bar Otonari in Tokyo.

viNever miss a tasting

The events calendar lists sake tastings, dinners, workshops and festivals in Singapore. You can filter by type and save the ones you want to go to.

viiA seat in two taps

Each event page shows the date, venue, price and who else is going. You can tap Going to let people know, or Reserve to book through the organiser.

viiiFollow people who actually know

Follow sommeliers, brewers and bar owners and see the bottles they are drinking and recommending right now.

ixFind your people

Search for friends by username and follow them. They approve your request before you’re connected, and you can talk sake with everyone in the community feed and forums.
