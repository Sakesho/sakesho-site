These four numbers decide where a sake sits in the app. Without them a bottle stays in the catalog but never gets recommended and never gets matched to a dish.

Three of the six are printed on the back label. Only SMV, acidity and yeast need the brewery.

Two ways to give us that

Spreadsheet

Several people, or forwarding to Japan

We send a sheet with your 22 bottles already listed. Grey columns are what we hold, so you can check we matched the right bottle. Cream columns are the gaps. Fill them in and email it back.

Leave blank anything you do not have. Nothing gets estimated.

Online form

One person working through the list

A link that opens with your 22 bottles already in it. It checks as you go, tells you which bottles are complete, takes photos, and sets the taste scales. Download the file at the end and email it over.

It saves to one browser on one computer, so it suits one person rather than a team.

Simplest option

Least work for you

Send us whatever spec sheets you already hold from the seven breweries. PDFs, product sheets, a photo of the back label. We read the numbers out and enter them ourselves.

If a brewery has no SMV or acidity

Some kura do not publish them. In that case someone who has drunk the sake can set the four taste scales by hand.

The form has a single screen with all 22 bottles on it, four scales each, and takes about 20 minutes. Scores we have already calculated are marked and left alone, so most bottles need three, not four.

Bottle photos

Usable at full size — 2 Web thumbnails — 20

The photos on your site are the right kind — bottle upright on clean white. They are just too small: 20 of the 22 are between 157 and 338 pixels wide, and we need at least 500 × 800.

  • The originals these were made from, 1000 pixels tall or more
  • A Drive or Dropbox folder is fine, named by SKU if possible
  • Whole bottle, cap to base
  • No need to cut them out or remove backgrounds — we do that

How this works

  • Nothing goes live automatically. The file comes to us, we check it, then it publishes.
  • Every bottle has a sell or hold switch, so allocation stock can sit in the catalog without being orderable.
  • Trade price is never shown publicly.
  • Partial is fine. We load what is ready and come back for the rest.
  • Your descriptions stay yours. We may shorten for space, we will not rewrite them.
